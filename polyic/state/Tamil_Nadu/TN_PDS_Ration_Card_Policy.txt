Tamil Nadu Public Distribution System (PDS) and Ration Card Policy

Overview of Tamil Nadu PDS:
Tamil Nadu Government implements the Universal Public Distribution System (UPDS). No exclusion is made based on income criteria. The Chief Minister made the universal public distribution system 'poor friendly' by ordering rice at free of cost under the public distribution system to all eligible card holders from 01.06.2011. Under the Public Distribution System, rice is distributed free of cost. Wheat, Sugar and Kerosene are being distributed at subsidised prices. Application form for applying for a new family card has been prescribed by the Government and can be downloaded from the Civil Supplies Department website www.consumer.tn.gov.in.

Ration Card Types and Commodity Entitlements in Tamil Nadu:
There are three types of ration cards issued in Tamil Nadu:
1. All Commodity cards (Green colour): Issued to cardholders opting for rice as well as all other essential commodities.
2. Sugar cards (White): Issued to cardholders opting for sugar instead of rice. Sugar card holders can buy all other essential commodities except rice, and are eligible to get an additional 3 kg of sugar in lieu of rice.
3. No commodity cards (White): Issued to cardholders who do not want any commodity under the Public Distribution System.

Commodity Entitlements under Tamil Nadu PDS:
1. Rice: Free of cost since 01.06.2011. All rice card holders are eligible for 5 kg of rice per person (including a child) per month or erstwhile entitlement whichever is higher. All AAY (Antyodaya Anna Yojana) cards are supplied with 35 kg per month at the rate of Rs 2 per kg. Scale: 4 kg per adult and 2 kg per child, subject to a minimum of 12 kg and a maximum of 20 kg per ration card. For Nilgiris district, an additional quantity of 4 kg of rice is issued per ration card.
2. Sugar: Rs 13.50 per kg for AAY cards and Rs 25 for all other cards. Scale: 500 grams per head per month, subject to a maximum of 2 kg per month. Sugar option cardholders get an additional 3 kg of sugar, subject to a maximum of 5 kg per month.
3. Wheat: Rs 7.50 per kg. Scale: 10 kg per card per month in Chennai City and District Head Quarters, 5 kg per card per month in other areas, subject to availability.
4. Kerosene: The scale depends on the number of LPG cylinders the cardholder possesses.

Eligibility for New Ration Card in Tamil Nadu:
The following rules apply for getting a new ration card:
1. The applicant and family must be Indian citizens.
2. The applicant and family must be living and cooking separately.
3. The applicant and family must be ordinarily resident in Tamil Nadu.
4. The applicant or family members must not possess any family card in any State in India.
5. The applicant or any family members must not be a member in any other family card in Tamil Nadu.
6. The applicant and members of the family must be close relatives.

How to Apply for Ration Card in Tamil Nadu:
Government of Tamil Nadu has prescribed an application format in English and Tamil, available on the Civil Supplies Department website. The application must be printed, filled completely, signed or thumb-impressed, and submitted with required documents.

Where to Submit Application:
- In Chennai city and belt areas: Submit to the Assistant Commissioner of Civil Supplies of the zone.
- In other districts: Submit to the Taluk Supply Officer (TSO) or Assistant Rationing Officer (in Coimbatore city).
- Applications can also be submitted online through the e-District/e-Sevai portal (PDS-501).

Required Documents for Ration Card Application:
1. Proof of current residence in Tamil Nadu (copy of original): Election Photo Identity Card (EPIC), OR Property tax receipt (current year), OR Last month's electricity bill, OR Last month's telephone bill, OR Front page of bank passbook, OR Allotment orders from Slum Clearance Board, OR Passport, OR Currently valid tenancy agreement.
2. Surrender Certificate or Name Deletion Certificate or No Card Certificate from previous address.
3. Details of LPG connection (consumer name, number, agency, Oil Company).
4. Mobile number or email address for status updates.

Ration Card Processing Timeline:
- Field inspection must be done within 30 days of filing.
- Government has fixed the time limit for passing orders on applications as 60 days from the date of application.
- Fee for a new ration card: Rs 5.

Antyodaya Anna Yojana (AAY) in Tamil Nadu:
The poorest of the poor are targeted for issue of Antyodaya Anna Yojana cards. AAY cards are eligible for 35 kg of rice per month, distributed free of cost. Despite the Government of India stipulating a cost of Rs 3 per kg for rice under this scheme, the Tamil Nadu Government provides it free.

Food Security under National Food Security Act (NFSA):
Under the NFSA, Tamil Nadu provides subsidized food grains through its PDS network. The state has implemented universal coverage, meaning all families can access the PDS regardless of income level.

Fair Price Shops:
Tamil Nadu operates a network of Fair Price Shops (FPS) for distribution of essential commodities under PDS. Issue of family cards to eligible families is a continuous process, and the Government has issued instructions to issue cards without delay while eliminating bogus cards.

Appeal Process:
If an application is rejected, the applicant can appeal to:
- Deputy Commissioner of Civil Supplies, Chennai (for Chennai city and belt areas)
- District Revenue Officer of the district concerned (for other areas)

Online Services (e-District Portal):
Tamil Nadu offers several PDS services online through the e-District portal:
- PDS-501: Application for New Ration Card
- PDS-502: Addition of New Family Member in Ration Card
- PDS-503: Family Head Member Change
- PDS-504: Removal of Family Member in Ration Card
- PDS-505: Change of Address in Ration Card
- PDS-506: Re-Print of Smart Card Application
